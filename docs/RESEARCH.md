# Research & Verification Log

**Research date: September 5, 2026.** Methodology: every candidate was checked
against its **official domain** (page fetch or verified press/platform source).
Search-engine results were used only to locate official pages and to confirm
current status; clone/"unblocked" mirror sites were never linked.

The ecosystem moves fast — this log records what was true on the research date
and why entries were included or excluded.

## 4. Network Check test targets

`data/network-tests.json` lists 30 gaming domains plus two controls:

- **Positive control**: `https://example.com/` — IANA-reserved, should always resolve.
- **Negative control**: `https://network-check-demo.invalid/` — `.invalid` is
  reserved by RFC 2606 and can never resolve; it demonstrates the red state
  without touching any third-party host.

A third, implicit control (this portal's own origin) is tested at runtime; if
it fails, all results are downgraded to "unable to determine".

## 5. Verification principles used

1. **Official domains only** — no mirrors, no "unblocked" clones.
2. **No fabricated statistics** — game counts are shown only when attributable
   (platform claims, Wikipedia, or press), and always marked `~`/approx.
3. **Honest status notes** — dead-ish games (Krunker), freemium limits
   (GeoGuessr, NYT Games, Kagi) and account walls are labelled, not hidden.
4. **Popularity is an editorial 1–3 estimate**, not a measured metric.
5. **Dates are per-entry** (`lastVerified`) so future maintainers know exactly
   how fresh each row is.
